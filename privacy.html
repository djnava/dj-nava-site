Privacy Policy

DJ NAVA LLC. Last updated October 20, 2026.

This policy explains what information DJ NAVA LLC ("DJ Nava", "we") collects when you reach out about booking, and how it is used.

What we collect

When you fill out a booking form on officialdjnava.com, or a lead form on Instagram or Facebook, we collect what you choose to give us: your name, email, phone number, event date, location or venue, event type and any message you include.

Our website also uses the Meta Pixel to measure how people find us and whether our ads are working. It may collect basic technical data such as pages visited and device type.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your inquiry, check availability, send a quote and plan your event. We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.

Who can see it

Only DJ Nava. Form submissions made through Instagram or Facebook are also processed by Meta under Meta's privacy policy. If you book, we may share event details with the venue or planner you work with, only as needed for your event.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details as long as needed to handle your booking and our business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we have about you, or to stop hearing from us, email bookings@officialdjnava.com.
